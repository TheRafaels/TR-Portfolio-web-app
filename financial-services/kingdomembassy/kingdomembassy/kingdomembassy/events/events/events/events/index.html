Cross over night

Crossover Night is our most anticipated annual gathering, taking place every December 31st from sunset until sunrise. This powerful, all-night service is dedicated to closing out the old year with intense gratitude and crossing over into the new year with prophetic prayers, dynamic worship, and spiritual alignment. We gather as a church family to break old limitations, declare breakthroughs, and receive spiritual direction for the incoming year. Do not step into the new year alone, join us for an unforgettable night of divine transition, praise, and new beginnings.

WE ARE KINGDOM EMBASSY CENTRE

We are not just a church; we are a gathering of ambassadors called to represent the authority, love, and culture of the Kingdom of Heaven here on earth. Kingdom Embassy Centre Zambia is a dynamic, multi-generational ministry dedicated to transforming lives, restoring families, and empowering individuals to walk in their God-given destiny. Anchored firmly on uncompromised biblical truth, explosive worship, and the supernatural power of the Holy Spirit, we exist to establish God's mandate in our communities and across the nations. This is a place where you will be equipped to rule in life, grow in faith, and make an eternal impact.
2 Corinthians 5:20 "Now then, we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were pleading through us: we implore you on Christ’s behalf, be reconciled to God."

Our Purpose.

Our ultimate mandate is to fulfill the Great Commission by raising transformed, mature disciples who actively demonstrate the reality and power of God's Kingdom. We exist to anchor individuals in uncompromised biblical truth, rescue the broken through the message of salvation, and equip every believer to govern and excel in their unique spheres of influence. Through authentic community, continuous spiritual growth, and cultural impact, our purpose is to manifest the love of Christ, advance His sovereign rule across Zambia, and leave a lasting spiritual legacy for generations to come.
Matthew 28:19-20 "Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and lo, I am with you always, even to the end of the age."

Our Mission.

Our mission is to actively preach the uncompromised gospel of Jesus Christ, rescue the lost, and transform lives through the power of the Holy Spirit. We are committed to establishing a strong spiritual foundation where individuals are healed, delivered, and deeply rooted in the Word of God. By intentionally building vibrant communities, mentoring leaders, and expanding our reach across Zambia, we fulfill our heavenly assignment to manifest the dominion, love, and righteousness of the Kingdom in every sector of society.
Luke 4:18-19 "The Spirit of the Lord is upon Me, because He has anointed Me to preach the gospel to the poor; He has sent Me to heal the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives and recovery of sight to the blind, to set at liberty those who are oppressed; to proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord."

Our Values.

Identity in Christ

Your true value, purpose, and worth are not defined by the opinions of the world, your past mistakes, or your current circumstances; they are fully established in Jesus Christ. As a child of God, you are completely loved, securely forgiven, and intentionally chosen to walk in supernatural victory. When you understand your divine identity, you stop fighting for victory and begin living from the victory that has already been won for you on the cross. At Kingdom Embassy Centre, we stand firmly on this truth, helping every believer break free from insecurity and step boldly into the royal inheritance, authority, and righteousness belonging to them as citizens of the Kingdom.
1 Peter 2:9 "But you are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, His own special people, that you may proclaim the praises of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light."

Church Is Family

We believe that church is much more than a weekly event or a building; it is a spiritual home where you truly belong. At Kingdom Embassy Centre, we are a family knit together by the love of Jesus Christ, walking shoulder to shoulder through every season of life. This is a safe haven where you can find authentic relationships, share your burdens, celebrate your victories, and grow in your faith alongside brothers and sisters who genuinely care for you. No matter your background, age, or story, you are not alone—you have a place at our table and a home in our hearts.
Ephesians 2:19 "Now, therefore, you are no longer strangers and foreigners, but fellow citizens with the saints and members of the household of God."

Kingdom Culture

True kingdom culture is the active manifestation of Heaven's values, atmosphere, and lifestyle right here on earth. At Kingdom Embassy Centre, we refuse to conform to the patterns, trends, and toxic standards of the world. Instead, we intentionally cultivate an environment of absolute honor, unwavering integrity, radical generosity, and profound love. It is a culture where the supernatural is natural, where service is a privilege, and where righteousness drives our decisions. By living out these celestial principles daily, we transform our families, excel in our professions, and establish a shining standard that draws our communities closer to the heart of God.
Romans 14:17 "For the kingdom of God is not eating and drinking, but righteousness and peace and joy in the Holy Spirit."

Spirit Led Life

True kingdom living is impossible without a complete reliance on the Person, presence, and power of the Holy Spirit. A Spirit-led life is not about human effort or religious routine; it is a lifestyle of daily submission, sensitivity, and obedience to the voice of God. At Kingdom Embassy Centre, we believe that the Holy Spirit is our divine Guide, Comforter, and Counselor, who empowers us to overcome trials, unlocks supernatural wisdom, and produces Christ-like character within us. When you surrender your plans to His leading, your steps become divinely ordered, your decisions are seasoned with heaven's insight, and you begin to walk in the miraculous, victorious reality designed for every child of God.
Romans 8:14 "For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are sons of God."


What We Believe.

WORSHIP

At Kingdom Embassy Church, worship is not just a segment of our service or a collection of songs, it is our lifestyle and our highest calling. We believe that true worship is a powerful, spirit-led encounter that shifts atmospheres, breaks chains, and aligns our hearts with Heaven. As we lift our voices in absolute surrender, gratitude, and praise, we create a dwelling place for Gods tangible presence to heal, transform, and restore everyone who walks through our doors Scripture Reference: "But the hour is coming, and is now here, when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth, for the Father is seeking such people to worship him. John 4:23

PRAYER

We believe prayer is the spiritual engine of our faith and our direct lifeline to the heart of God. It is not a religious ritual, but a powerful conversation that shifts realities, unlocks heavenly blueprints, and releases supernatural breakthroughs. We stand on the truth that a praying church is a powerful church, and when we call upon the Lord in unity, He hears us, answers us, and moves mightily across our families and our nation. 1st Thessalonians 5:17-18: Pray without ceasing, in everything give thanks; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus

THE WORD AND BIBLICAL TEACHINGS

'Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path' Psalm 119:105. The Word of God is our ultimate authority, our immovable anchor, and the absolute foundation for everything we do. We do not just read the Scriptures; we preach and teach them with bold conviction, uncompromised truth, and practical wisdom for everyday living. We believe that Gods Word is alive, powerful, and sharp capable of transforming minds, healing broken lives, and equipping every believer to walk in victory and divine purpose 2 Timothy 3:16 All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness

THE PROPHETIC

We lean into the timely, breathing voice of God that brings alignment, comfort, and divine direction. We believe the prophetic is a powerful gift intended to reveal God's heart, unlock hidden potential, and point hearts back to Jesus. By cultivating an environment that honors Biblical prophecy, we move forward with supernatural clarity, boldly declaring His future over our families, our church, and the nation of Zambia. Amos 3:7

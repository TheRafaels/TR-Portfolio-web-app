Join the Team

At Kingdom Embassy Centre, we believe that every individual carries a unique, God-given gift meant to bless and build the body of Christ. Serving is not a chore, it is an incredible privilege, an act of worship, and a powerful way to discover your purpose while creating a home for others. When you join a volunteer team, you step out of the seats and into the story of what God is doing across Zambia. Whether you are welcoming visitors at the door, operating the media desk, or teaching the next generation, your hands and your heart help make the miraculous possible. Find your place, share your passion, and lets serve our King together.

"As each has received a gift, use it to serve one another, as good stewards of God's varied grace." 1 Peter 4:10 For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many." Mark 10:45


Volunteer With Us Through the Following Ministries

Ushering

Our Ushering Team serves as the very first face of Kingdom Embassy Center, carrying a mandate to welcome every person with the radical warmth, love, and honor of Christ. More than just opening doors or directing seating, our ushers are spiritual gatekeepers who intentionally set an atmosphere of peace and order from the moment you arrive. By managing the sanctuary with care and excellence, this team ensures that every service flows smoothly, allowing the congregation to focus entirely on encountering the presence of God. Psalm 84:10 "For a day in your courts is better than a thousand elsewhere. I would rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God than dwell in the tents of wickedness." Romans 15:7 "Therefore welcome one another as Christ has welcomed you, for the glory of God."

Kids Church.

"Our Kids Church team is passionately dedicated to raising the next generation of spiritual giants, dynamic leaders, and faithful disciples of Jesus Christ. We dont just watch over children, we intentionally invest in them through vibrant, age-appropriate Bible lessons, high-energy worship, and fun, interactive activities. Our goal is to create a safe, joyful environment where every child discovers their true identity in Christ, learns to hear the voice of the Holy Spirit, and builds a firm foundation of faith that will anchor them for the rest of their lives Proverbs 22:6 "Train up a child in the way he should go; even when he is old he will not depart from it." Matthew 19:14 "But Jesus said, 'Let the little children come to me and do not hinder them, for to such belongs the kingdom of heaven."

Music Team.

Our Music Team carries a unique mantle to lead the congregation into the very gates of Heaven through prophetic, spirit-led praise and deep worship. More than just skilled singers and musicians, this team is comprised of active worshipers who spend time in the secret place so they can minister with spiritual authority and raw authenticity on the altar. By preparing the atmosphere with musical excellence and absolute devotion, they help break heavy chains, heal heavy hearts, and build a glorious dwelling place for the tangible presence of God to move during every service. Psalm 33:3 "Sing to him a new song; play skillfully on the strings, with a loud shout." John 4:24 "God is spirit, and those who worship him must worship in spirit and truth."

Prayer Team.

Our Intercessory Prayer Team serves as the spiritual shield and engine of Rhema Life Church. This dedicated team stands faithfully in the gap, lifting up the vision of the house, our pastoral leadership, the needs of the congregation, and the transformation of the nation of Zambia. Moving in spiritual discernment and unwavering faith, our prayer warriors meet regularly to wage warfare, break spiritual strongholds, and release heavenly breakthroughs so that the atmosphere remains clear and open for God’s power to move. Ephesians 6:18 "Praying at all times in the Spirit, with all prayer and supplication. To that end, keep alert with all perseverance, making supplication for all the saints." James 5:16 Therefore I confess my sins to one another and pray for one another, that you may be healed. The prayer of a righteous person has great power as it works.

Administrative Support.

Our Administrative Support Team is the logistical anchor of Rhema Life Church, executing the operations of the ministry with deep integrity, excellence, and order. True ministry requires both spiritual power and structural wisdom, and this dedicated team works diligently behind the scenes to handle communications, coordinate church schedules, manage data, and support daily workflows. By operating with high efficiency, they create the firm framework that allows our pastors to minister effectively and ensures that every member of our church family is cared for smoothly. 1 Corinthians 14:40 "But all things should be done decently and in order." 1 Corinthians 14:33 "For God is not a God of confusion but of peace."

Media team and Production Design.

Our Media and Production Design Team utilizes technology, creativity, and visual excellence to project the message of the Gospel far beyond the physical walls of our building. From operating sound systems and programming dynamic lighting to managing multi-camera video production and streaming services online, this team sets the technical stage for encounters with God. By pursuing absolute excellence in audio, video, and design, they ensure that the Word preached and the worship lifted are carried clearly, seamlessly, and powerfully to everyone in the room and across the globe. Psalm 33:3 "Sing to him a new song; play skillfully on the strings, with a loud shout." Psalm 96:3 "Declare his glory among the nations, his marvelous works among all the peoples!"

Technical & Stage Management.

Our Technical and Stage Management Team operates as the seamless command center behind every service, ensuring structural alignment, timing, and smooth transitions on the altar. Working hand-in-hand with pastoral leadership and the media departments, this team manages stage logistics, props, mic placements, and service cues with strict precision. By removing logistical friction and protecting the flow of the service, they allow the ministry to move forward without distraction, creating an orderly space where the Holy Spirit can flow freely. 1 Corinthians 14:40 "But all things should be done decently and in order." Proverbs 22:29 "Do you see a man skillful in his work? He will stand before kings; he will not stand before obscure men."

Security & Safety Team

Our Security Team serves with quiet vigilance, commitment, and strength to ensure that Rhema Life Church remains a safe, peaceful sanctuary for everyone who enters. Operating with high awareness and professionalism, this team watches over our premises, coordinates parking structures, and manages safety protocols during services and special events. By establishing a secure environment, they provide total peace of mind, allowing our pastoral leadership, families, and visitors to worship, fellowship, and receive the Word of God without distraction or worry. Psalm 127:1 "Unless the Lord builds the house, those who build it labor in vain. Unless the Lord watches over the city, the watchman stays awake in vain." 1 Corinthians 16:13 "Be on your guard; stand firm in the faith; be courageous; be strong."

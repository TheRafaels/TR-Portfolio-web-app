Youth Conference

Our annual Youth Conference is the ultimate weekend event for teenagers and young adults seeking to discover their true identity and purpose. This high-octane gathering is packed with dynamic live worship, inspiring guest speakers, competitive team challenges, and practical breakout tracks that tackle the real issues today's youth face. It is more than just a weekend event—it is a movement designed to help young people break free from distractions, build life-changing friendships, and walk away fully equipped to live out a bold, uncompromised faith in their schools and communities.

Welcome to Rhema Life Church

We are so thrilled that you have found your way to Rhema Life Church! Whether you are visiting our website for the first time, looking for a spiritual family in Zambia, or seeking a deeper relationship with God, we want you to know that you are welcome here. We believe that your arrival is not an accident, but a divine appointment. At Rhema Life Church, you will experience the living, uncompromised Word of God, dynamic spirit-led worship, and a loving community that instantly feels like family. Come just as you are, discover the incredible destiny God has mapped out for your life, and grow with us in His grace. We cannot wait to fellowship with you.

Shape Your Life in the realm of worship

At Rhema Life Church, worship is our heartbeat and our highest privilege. We invite you to join us as we step out of the noise of everyday life and enter into a powerful, spirit-led encounter with the Living God. Our services feature passionate, contemporary praise and intimate worship that shifts atmospheres, breaks chains, and brings healing. This isn't just a routine or a performance, it is a space where the tangible presence of God is free to move, transform hearts, and speak to your life. Whether you come to lift your voice, find peace, or seek a fresh breakthrough, you are welcome to encounter Him here.

If you are searching for more than just a routine Sunday service, you will find it at Rhema Life Church. We are a vibrant, forward thinking community where the timeless truth of the Bible meets real, everyday life. People love Rhema because it feels like a modern spiritual family a place where you are genuinely noticed, warmly welcomed, and supported on your journey. Whether you are seeking deep community, an uplifting environment for your children, or a fresh spiritual breakthrough, Rhema Life Church is a place where you can experience the tangible presence of God and build a life of true victory.

We Are Rhema Life Church

Welcome to Rhema Life Church, where the living Word of God transforms hearts and empowers lives. Here in Zambia, we are dedicated to raising a generation anchored in faith, walking in purpose, and shining the light of Christ across our communities. No matter where you are on your spiritual journey, there is a place for you to belong, grow, and experience the fullness of Gods grace. Join us as we worship, serve, and build a brighter future together on the foundation of His truth.

Our Purpose.

At Rhema Life Church, our purpose is to actively anchor communities in the living Word of God, transforming hearts and empowering believers across Zambia to walk boldly in their divine destinies. We exist to be a beacon of hope and grace equipping individuals to grow in faith, share the love of Christ, and shine His light into every home and community. Together, we are building a lasting legacy of spiritual excellence, grounded firmly in His unchanging truth.

Our Mission.

Our mission at Rhema Life Church is to preach the uncompromised Word of God, make devoted disciples of Jesus Christ, and impact the world with the power of the Gospel. We are committed to fostering a vibrant family of faith where believers are spiritually matured, families are strengthened, and leaders are raised to bring transformation and hope to every sector of society.

For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life. For God did not send his Son into the world to condemn the world, but in order that the world might be saved through him.

John 3:16-17 ESV

Our Values.

Identity in Christ

2 Corinthians 5:17 At Rhema Life Church, we believe you are exactly who God says you are deeply loved, completely forgiven, and purposefully created. You are not defined by your past or your circumstances, but by your identity as a chosen child of the Living God. Here, we walk boldly in the righteousness of Christ, empowered by His Spirit to live a life of victory, authority, and unbreakable hope. Ephesians 2:10

Church Is Family

At Rhema Life Church, you are never a stranger, and you never walk alone, you are family. We believe that church is more than just a weekly building we visit; it is a vibrant, loving home where we share life's joys, bear one another's burdens, and grow together in God's grace. Here, every generation finds a place to belong, a community to support them, and a spiritual family where we love deeply, serve joyfully, and lift each other up.

Kingdom Culture

At Rhema Life Church, we look beyond the patterns of this world to cultivate a culture rooted in the eternal values of God's Kingdom. We intentionally build an atmosphere of honor, radical generosity, deep integrity, and selfless love that reflects Heaven here on Earth. By pursuing spiritual excellence and moving in the power of the Holy Spirit, we strive to make the presence of God tangible in our daily lives, transforming our workplaces, our homes, and our nation

Spirit Led Life

At Rhema Life Church, we don't rely on our own strength, but on the active power and whisper of the Holy Spirit. We believe a Spirit-led life is an adventurous journey of radical obedience, supernatural peace, and divine direction. By daily surrendering our steps to His guidance, we move with heavenly wisdom, operate in spiritual gifts, and watch God do the impossible in and through our lives across Zambia. Romans 8:14


What We Believe.

THE PROPHETIC.

At Rhema Life Church, we lean into the timely, breathing voice of God that brings alignment, comfort, and divine direction. We believe the prophetic is a powerful gift intended to reveal God's heart, unlock hidden potential, and point hearts back to Jesus. By cultivating an environment that honors Biblical prophecy, we move forward with supernatural clarity, boldly declaring His future over our families, our church, and the nation of Zambia. Amos 3:7

WORSHIP.

At Rhema Life Church, worship is not just a segment of our service or a collection of songs, it is our lifestyle and our highest calling. We believe that true worship is a powerful, spirit-led encounter that shifts atmospheres, breaks chains, and aligns our hearts with Heaven. As we lift our voices in absolute surrender, gratitude, and praise, we create a dwelling place for Gods tangible presence to heal, transform, and restore everyone who walks through our doors Scripture Reference: "But the hour is coming, and is now here, when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth, for the Father is seeking such people to worship him. John 4:23

PRAYER

At Rhema Life Church, we believe prayer is the spiritual engine of our faith and our direct lifeline to the heart of God. It is not a religious ritual, but a powerful conversation that shifts realities, unlocks heavenly blueprints, and releases supernatural breakthroughs. We stand on the truth that a praying church is a powerful church, and when we call upon the Lord in unity, He hears us, answers us, and moves mightily across our families and our nation. 1st Thessalonians 5:17-18: Pray without ceasing, in everything give thanks; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you.
